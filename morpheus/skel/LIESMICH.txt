What the factory image brings along in addition to the packages (06.11.2026):
  .config/matugen/        -> /etc/skel/.config/matugen   colour chain: wallpaper -> app palette + focus ring (niri palette.kdl)
  hintergruende/QUELLEN.txt -> /usr/share/backgrounds/matrix/QUELLEN.txt
The wallpapers themselves (24 images, Unsplash/Pexels/Pixabay, scaled down to 3000 px) are not in the repo;
QUELLEN.txt lists every image with its photographer and image ID, so they can be fetched again from the archives.
